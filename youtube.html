Classic YouTube

Developer: YouTube, Inc. (later acquired by Google)

First Release: February 14, 2005

Acquired by Google: November 2006

Classic YouTube featured a simple design with a white background, yellow star ratings, and easy navigation. It became the world's most popular video-sharing website and changed the way people watched and shared videos online.

Main Features

  • Simple classic interface
  • Video uploads by users
  • Comments and ratings
  • Favorite videos
  • Video sharing
  • User channels

History

The early versions of YouTube are remembered for their clean layout and simple design. Many users still feel nostalgic about the classic look before the modern redesigns introduced over the years.
