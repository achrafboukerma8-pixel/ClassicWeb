Internet Explorer

Developer: Microsoft

First Release: August 16, 1995

Final Version: Internet Explorer 11

Internet Explorer was Microsoft's first web browser and became one of the most popular browsers in the world during the late 1990s and early 2000s. It was included with Windows and introduced millions of people to the Internet.

Main Features

  • Tabbed Browsing (IE7 and later)
  • Favorites (Bookmarks)
  • Download Manager
  • Full-screen Browsing
  • Integrated with Windows
  • Support for many classic websites

History

Internet Explorer dominated the browser market for many years. It became a symbol of the early Internet era and was replaced by Microsoft Edge in 2015. Today, many people remember it with nostalgia because it was part of the history of the web.
