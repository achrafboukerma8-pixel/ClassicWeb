Google Chrome

Developer: Google

First Release: September 2, 2008

Current Status: One of the world's most popular web browsers.

Google Chrome was introduced by Google in 2008. It became famous for its speed, clean design, and excellent performance. Chrome changed the web browser market and quickly became the most widely used browser in the world.

Main Features

  • Fast browsing performance
  • Simple and modern interface
  • Automatic updates
  • Extensions support
  • Built-in translation
  • Synchronization with Google Account

History

The first version of Chrome introduced a new browser engine and a modern interface that influenced many browsers. Today, Chrome is available on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS.
