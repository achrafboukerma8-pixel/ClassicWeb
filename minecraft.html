Minecraft 1.0

Developer: Mojang Studios

Creator: Markus "Notch" Persson

Official Release: November 18, 2011

Minecraft 1.0 was the first official full release of Minecraft. It was announced during MineCon 2011 and marked the end of the beta development period. The game became one of the most successful video games in history.

Main Features

  • The End dimension
  • Ender Dragon boss
  • Enchanting system
  • Brewing and potions
  • Creative and Survival modes
  • Multiplayer support

History

Minecraft began as a small sandbox game in 2009 and quickly became a global phenomenon. Version 1.0 introduced many features that defined the game's future and attracted millions of players worldwide.

Fun Fact

Today, Minecraft is one of the best-selling video games of all time, with hundreds of millions of copies sold across many platforms.
